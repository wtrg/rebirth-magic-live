# Art Bible · Cổng Hư Vô

Original dark anime fantasy. A floating basalt arena with lavender runes and a magenta gate frames a horned raid guardian. Warm ivory type and rose boss HP are readable against deep violet. Fire reads orange, ice blue, thunder purple, knight rose, healer amber and summon jade.

Use a high three-quarter camera. The boss silhouette is twice the player scale. Make attack arcs directional and keep damage and HP legible in portrait. Use hard facets and emissive accents. Avoid full-screen bloom and costly dynamic shadows.
